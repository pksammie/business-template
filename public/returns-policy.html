1. Shipping & Delivery

Delivery fees are calculated automatically at checkout based on your state. Most orders are dispatched within 1-2 business days of being confirmed. You can track your order status any time from your Orders page.

2. Return Window

If something isn't right, you have 48 hours from the delivery date to let us know and start a return or exchange.

3. What Can Be Returned

  • Items that arrived damaged, defective, or significantly different from what was described
  • The wrong item or size was delivered
  • Unworn, unwashed items in their original condition with tags still attached

4. What Can't Be Returned

  • Items worn, washed, or altered from their original condition
  • Items returned after the 48-hour window without prior arrangement
  • Accessories and items marked as final sale, where applicable

5. How To Start A Return

Go to your Orders page, find the order in question, and use the complaint/support option — or message us directly on WhatsApp with your order details. Let us know what happened and whether you'd like a replacement or refund.

6. Refunds & Exchanges

Once we've received and checked the returned item, we'll process an exchange or refund within 3-5 business days. Refunds are sent to the same payment method or account used for the original order, unless otherwise agreed.

7. Damaged Or Wrong Items

If your item arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, please reach out within 48 hours with a photo of the item — this helps us resolve it as quickly as possible, at no extra cost to you.
