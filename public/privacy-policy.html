Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Timeless collects when you use this site, why we collect it, and how we protect it. By creating an account or placing an order with us, you agree to what's described here.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, browse, or place an order, we collect:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password (stored securely, never in plain text).
  • Order information: delivery address, phone number, and the products you purchase.
  • Communication: messages you send us through complaints, reviews, or WhatsApp.
  • Usage information: pages you visit and products you view, used to show you relevant items and improve the site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Communicate with you about order status, complaints, or account activity
  • Improve our catalog, site experience, and customer support
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse of the platform

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Who We Share Information With

We only share your information where it's genuinely necessary:

  • Delivery partners — your name, address, and phone number, solely to get your order to you.
  • Service providers we rely on to run the site (for example, our hosting and database provider), who are only permitted to use your data to provide that service.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or the safety of others.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your account and order data is stored with Google Firebase, a widely used cloud platform with industry-standard security practices. Access to customer and order data within our systems is restricted to store administration only. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You can at any time:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your account and associated data, subject to any legal obligation we have to retain order records

To make any of these requests, contact us using the details below.

6. Cookies

We use your browser's local storage to keep you logged in and to remember preferences like your selected theme. We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site in aggregate; this data does not identify you individually.

7. Children's Privacy

Timeless is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
